Every protocol against the Unnamed Instruments catalog, with what a negative result would kill and what it would leave standing. Nothing here has been run, so every design carries [inf] — a proposal, not a result. Sorting is by the plan's own rule: carrier-bound work sorts on expiry, not cost.

Standing rules — these bind every protocol below
1 · Two numbers or noneTrained carriers and naive controls, with the gap as the finding. And say which kind: a threshold difference or a reporting difference.
1b · Name the axis firstA trained population exceeds baseline on the axis it trains. A null on an unnamed axis is unreadable afterward.
2 · Method of loci firstThe positive control with an answer key. A protocol that can't recover a known orders-of-magnitude gap can't find a smaller one.
3 · Blind, or don't botherFelt externality is not evidence of externality. The carriers are not exempt by being right about other things.
4 · Score trial one apartEverything after the first trial is relearning — and calibration to an unfamiliar apparatus.
5 · Carrier priorityThese are not sample sizes. They are the entire population, and they are on the clock the whole repo is about.
