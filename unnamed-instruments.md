# Unnamed Instruments

A catalog of human sensing instruments that are physically real, in
use, and have no formal name — because nobody studied humans using
them deliberately. The proof case is proprioception in robotics: it
was uncodeable until someone said "that's a sense," and the naming was
the whole intervention. An unnamed instrument is invisible to any
system built by people who don't hold it.

This catalog does one thing per entry that separates it from
debunked-sensing claims (dowsing, EHS): it names the **operating
range** — the physical regime where the instrument works — and states
**what it cannot read**. A claim that specifies where it fails is a
measurement. A claim that works everywhere is a story.

Status: seed. Entries carry evidentiary tier. Real-and-quantified,
open-hypothesis, and misidentified-mechanism are different columns and
do not mix.

## Column A — transducers (physical detectors)

### A1. Arm-hair electroreception — fault detector

- **Reads:** elevated electric field at an open or arcing conductor —
  a short, a break, a fault. Read through drywall via hair-follicle
  mechanoreception (hairs move in the field).
- **Cannot read:** intact, functional wiring. The ambient field of an
  intact run behind drywall is ~1–50 V/m — orders of magnitude below
  the human perception threshold (kV/m-scale). It is correctly
  invisible. This is a **fault detector, not a wire detector.**
- **Operating range:** the fault is what lifts the local field into
  the readable band. Open/arcing conductors, corona discharge — local
  fields far above the intact-wiring ambient. Documented high-field
  cases where hair electroreception is real: thunderstorm ground
  fields (5–20 kV/m), carpet static (up to ~500 kV/m), HV transmission
  corridors (arm-hair vibration documented, e.g. BPA). [lit]
- **Why this survives where dowsing doesn't:** it specifies the regime
  it works in and concedes the regime it can't. Intact wiring below
  threshold is a *prediction the instrument makes about its own
  blindness*, and it's borne out. [inf]
- **Confound:** felt externality is not evidence of externality
  (dowsing/EHS/ideomotor lesson). Arbiter is a blinded test — cheap
  and shop-runnable: known faulted vs known-intact runs, operator
  blind to which. [lit/open]
- **Tier:** real mechanism, correct operating range. The prior
  "senses wiring through walls" framing failed its own numbers; the
  "senses faults" framing does not. [obs + lit]

### A2. Tool-mediated remote touch

- **Reads:** surface state, texture, vibration, and mechanical event
  *through a grasped tool* — engine condition through a wrench, cut
  state through a blade, material change through a probe.
- **Operating range:** Pacinian channel, ~200–250 Hz, sub-micron
  thresholds through the tool; grasping the tool lowers threshold by
  up to ~18.5 dB. Fully real and quantified. [lit]
- **Attack surface:** HAVS (hand-arm vibration syndrome) permanently
  destroys exactly this channel — ~12× Pacinian threshold elevation,
  irreversible at Stockholm-stage sensorineural damage. The
  practitioner's most valuable channel has a documented occupational
  kill mechanism. Protect it: vibration exposure limits. [lit]
- **Tier:** real, quantified, with a known degradation vector. [obs + lit]

### A3. Palm / skin radiometry

- **Reads:** radiant heat flux — a warm body, a live run, a forge, a
  hot bearing — at a distance, no contact.
- **Operating range:** detection floor ~6.7 W/m² (Hardy & Oppel 1937).
  Warm body at 10 cm ≈ 94 W/m²; forge at 2 m ≈ 220 W/m². Real
  radiometry. [lit]
- **Confound:** palm thresholds shift with adaptation (±°C, Filingeri
  2017) — the instrument re-zeros itself, so absolute readings drift.
  Self-deception risk; use differentials, not absolutes. [lit]
- **Related:** forge-color pyrometry (reading temperature off emitted
  color) is the same family, visual channel. [obs]
- **Tier:** real, quantified, adaptation confound noted. [obs + lit]

## Column B — representation formats

Not senses. Ways of holding what's sensed. They fail not at the
detector but at the interface — they die on contact with linear code
that demands a start, an end, and a single answer.

### B1. Probability-field representation

- Holding a state as a distribution rather than a committed value.
  Nothing closed; the small open fraction is where new hypothesis
  lives. The collapse to yes/no is in the *interface*, not the
  thinking — output forces a commitment the representation never made.
- **Serialization loss is measured, not rhetorical:** declarative
  procedures survive serialization; holistic perceptual fields degrade
  −4% to −25%; unconscious competence dies on contact. Bridge methods
  (structured elicitation, vector-symbolic computing) recover geometry
  but not nuance. [lit]
- **Tier:** format is real; the loss on serialization is measured. [obs + lit]

### B2. Geometric sensing

- Sensing in spatial/geometric relationships directly rather than
  through symbolic decomposition. Candidate unnamed format; less
  characterized than probability fields. [obs/open]
- **Tier:** open. Named here so it isn't lost; not yet specified.

## Column C — internal-state readout

Covered in full by emotion-reading-spec.md (content / amplitude /
impedance, plus the hormonal calibration layer). Listed here only so
the catalog is complete. The spec is the artifact; this is the
pointer.

- **Correction from Wave 3:** the calibration layer's *cycle-phase*
  scheduling is the weak version. Menstrual phase-based programming is
  not evidence-based (McNulty 2020 trivial ES; Colenso-Semple 2023 90%
  of quality studies null). The verified axis is **symptom reading**,
  not inferred phase — which is the direct-reading method anyway.
  Verified scheduling axis for the daily cycle: time-since-entrained-
  awakening (chronotype-anchored). [lit]
- **Literature hook:** the spec independently rediscovers Frijda's
  action-readiness / control-precedence model (verb-not-noun is
  verbatim Frijda). Content ≈ action-tendency aim; gain ≈ control
  precedence; threshold ≈ subthreshold→suprathreshold. Gives the spec
  a citation lineage. [lit]
- **Open counter:** content/gain separability is a pragmatic
  partition, not established as a natural kind (Russell core-affect
  integral-blend argument; Nook 2021 labeling-can-impede-reappraisal).
  Testable, currently unproven. [open]

## The frame — naming is the intervention

Every entry here is uncodeable by any system whose builders don't hold
the instrument. Proprioception in robotics is the resolved case: the
field built limbs with no idea where they were until the sense was
named, and naming was the entire fix. The catalog exists because the
same failure is running now on the instruments above, invisibly, in
systems built from the chair.

An unnamed instrument that is also transmitted only through practice
(Column C) is on a carrier clock — see tool-off-metrology and
emotion-reading-spec. Cataloguing it is the first move off that clock.

## Evidentiary tiers

- **real-quantified** — mechanism and operating range documented
  (A1, A2, A3, B1, C)
- **open-hypothesis** — plausible, not established; do not file next to
  real-quantified. Human magnetoreception sits here: Wang 2019 has zero
  independent replications 2019–2026, unique apparatus; brain magnetite
  is real but largely exogenous pollution, no receptor organ found.
  Named, quarantined, not catalogued as a working instrument. [lit]
- **misidentified-mechanism** — a real reading attributed to the wrong
  channel. The residual, not a debunk. (Where A1 started before the
  operating range was pinned to faults.)
